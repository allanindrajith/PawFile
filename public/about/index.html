PawFile

One shared health record for your pet

PawFile is a web app for pet owners and veterinary clinics.

PawFile keeps a pet's health history in one place: vaccinations, allergies, medications, clinical notes, photos and reminders. Instead of each clinic holding a separate paper file, the owner has a single record that follows the pet, and a vet sees what they need at the moment they need it.

Open PawFile

What PawFile does

  • Owner-controlled sharing. Pet owners decide which clinic can see their pet's record. Access to a record is protected by the owner's PIN.
  • One record, every clinic. Vaccinations, allergies, medications and visit notes stay together and are available to any clinic the owner has approved.
  • Access is logged. Every time a record is viewed, it is recorded together with who viewed it.
  • Reminders and chat. Owners get vaccination reminders and can message their vet.

Who it is for

Pet owners who want their pet's history in one place, and veterinary clinics who want accurate, up-to-date records without chasing paperwork.
