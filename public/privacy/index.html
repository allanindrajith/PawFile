Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

PawFile is a pet health record app for veterinary clinics and pet owners. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, used only to create and identify your account.
  • Content you add: pet profiles, photos, vaccination and clinical records, reminders, and chat messages between owners and vets.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run PawFile: showing your pets' records to you and to the clinic you are linked with, and sending reminders. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting and database provider, Supabase, and protected by access rules so that users can only see records they are linked to. Sign-in is handled by Google.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated records at any time by emailing the address below.
