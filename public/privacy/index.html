PawFile Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

PawFile (pawfile.itsallan.me) is a web app that lets pet owners and veterinary clinics keep and share a pet's health record. This policy explains what information PawFile collects, why, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

From your Google account. When you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares with PawFile your name, email address and profile picture, along with a unique account identifier. PawFile requests only these basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile). It does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.

Information you provide. Depending on whether you are a pet owner or a vet, you may add: your role and phone number; pet details (name, breed, date of birth, photos); vaccination, allergy, medication and clinical records; reminders; clinic details; a PIN used to control sharing; and chat messages between owners and vets.

Usage records. When a pet's record is viewed, PawFile logs who viewed it and when, so owners can see who has accessed their pet's information.

Technical data. To keep you signed in, your browser stores a session token. PawFile may also keep a small amount of data in your browser for offline use. PawFile does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. How we use information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To show you your pets' records and, with the owner's permission, to the clinics they choose.
  • To send in-app reminders (for example, upcoming vaccinations) and to deliver chat messages.
  • To keep the service secure, including the access log and PIN protection, and to prevent abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Google user data

PawFile's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google name, email and profile picture are used only to provide the account and profile features described above. They are not used for advertising, are not sold or transferred to third parties other than as described in this policy, and are not read by people except where needed for support, security or to comply with law.

4. Who can see your information

  • You. You can see your own account and your pets' records.
  • Clinics you approve. A clinic can view a pet's record only when the pet's owner has shared it, protected by the owner's PIN. Vets in a clinic see their clinic's records.
  • Service providers. PawFile uses Supabase to host its database, file storage and authentication, and Vercel to host the website. These providers process data only to run the service for us. Sign-in is performed by Google.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law.

We do not share your information with anyone else.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in a database protected by row-level access rules, so that signed-in users can only read records they own or have been given access to. PINs are stored as hashes, not in plain text, with limits on repeated wrong attempts. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please keep your PIN private.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and records for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data (profile, pet records, photos and messages) at any time by emailing allanindrajith@gmail.com. We will act on the request within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. Data held in provider backups is removed on their normal backup cycle. You can also revoke PawFile's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how it is used, by contacting us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data-protection law.

8. Children

PawFile is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. We rely on those providers' safeguards when data is transferred.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.

11. Contact

PawFile, operated by Allan Indrajith. Questions, requests or complaints: allanindrajith@gmail.com
